Is the trial lesson really free?

Yes. Your first lesson is free, with no payment details and no obligation. If it isn't the right fit, you simply walk away.

Which subjects and curriculums do you cover?

Maths, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Statistics, English, Economics and Computer Science. We teach the American curriculum (including AP), Indian CBSE and ISC, British IGCSE, GCSE, O Level and A Level, IB, Australian Years 4–12 (NAPLAN, HSC, VCE) and university courses.

Do you prepare students for entry tests?

Yes. We prepare students for the SAT, IMAT, BMAT and UCAT, from full prep courses to last-minute crash courses, with timed practice papers and score tracking. Please check your target university's current requirements, as some no longer accept every test.

How do you choose my child's tutor?

We don't leave it to chance or a search filter. Our team matches a tutor to the subject, curriculum or test, your child's level and learning style, and your preferred times. If the fit isn't right, we'll switch tutors for free.

Where are you based, and who do you teach?

We're based in the UAE and teach students all over the world. Most of our students are in the Gulf (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain and Oman), the UK, Australia, New Zealand, the USA and Canada. Lessons are always scheduled in your own timezone.

How do online lessons work?

Lessons run live, one-to-one, by video call with a shared whiteboard. All you need is a laptop or tablet with a camera and a stable internet connection.

Can we cancel or reschedule a lesson?

Yes. Reschedule or cancel any lesson with at least 24 hours' notice, and the hour stays in your balance.

How will I know it's working?

You'll get notes after every lesson and a written progress report every month showing strengths, gaps and the plan ahead. For entry tests, we also share your practice-test scores.

Can I teach with SAS Tutors?

Yes. Tap "Teach with us" in the menu to apply. We review every application, then hold a short interview and a demo lesson.

How many hours have you taught?

More than 5,000 hours of one-to-one lessons so far.

How much do lessons cost?

Is the plan tied to a subject, tutor or curriculum?

No. Your hours work for any subject, any tutor on our team, and any school, university, curriculum or board. You can mix and match as you go.

No. Hours you buy never expire.
