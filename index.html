Is the trial lesson really free?

Yes. Your first lesson is free, with no card details and no obligation. If it isn't the right fit, you simply walk away.

How do you choose my child's tutor?

We don't leave it to chance or a search filter. Our team matches a tutor to the subject, the curriculum, your child's year and learning style, and your preferred times. If the fit isn't right, we'll switch tutors for free.

How do online lessons work?

Lessons run live, one-to-one, by video call with a shared whiteboard. All you need is a laptop or tablet with a camera. Lessons are scheduled in your own timezone.

Which subjects and curriculums do you cover?

Maths, English and the sciences from Years 4–12, including NAPLAN, selective school preparation, HSC, VCE and ACT, plus IGCSE, GCSE, A-Level and IB.

Can we cancel or reschedule a lesson?

Yes. Reschedule or cancel with at least 24 hours' notice. There are no lock-in contracts.

How will I know it's working?

You'll get notes after every lesson and a written progress report every month showing strengths, gaps and the plan ahead.
